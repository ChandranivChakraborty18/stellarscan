From starlight to a decision

Big telescopes have limited time. This is the triage step before that — sorting signal from noise, and noise from mystery.

Classify

A trained model reads ten measurements of a signal and predicts whether it's a confirmed planet, a candidate, or a false positive.

Score

Every result gets a plain 0–100 priority score, so nobody has to guess what "confident" means.

Detect

A separate model checks if the signal statistically resembles anything else we've measured — or stands apart.

Flag

Unusual signals are surfaced clearly, so a human decides what deserves a closer look.
